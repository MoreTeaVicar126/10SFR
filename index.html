Brody's Biscuits

Swap neighbouring treats to match three or more. Line up three chocolate biscuits to clear the pile. Stuck? Answer a fractions question to earn a lifeline.

Play
How to install on your phone

iPhone or iPad (use Safari)

  1. Open this page in Safari and tap Play on Brody's Biscuits.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. The biscuit icon is now on your home screen.

Android (use Chrome)

  1. Open this page in Chrome and tap Play on Brody's Biscuits.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top corner.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  4. Tap Install. The biscuit icon is now on your home screen.

Good to know

  • Install from inside the game itself, not from this page, so the right icon is added. Tap Play first, then follow the steps.
  • Open the link in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android). If you tapped it in a messaging or school app, use Open in browser first.
  • The game needs the internet the first time you open it. After that it works offline.
  • Games remember things like best scores and settings on your own phone, so they won't appear on anyone else's.
