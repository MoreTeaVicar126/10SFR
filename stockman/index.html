How it works

Move Max with the arrow keys / WASD (diagonals: Q E Z C, or the number pad). Space or 5 waits a turn. Every move is one turn.

Cows are drawn from above. The head (with the pink nose) is the front and the tail is the rear, and she faces the way she last walked. When Max is in one of the 8 squares around a cow:

  • Behind it (the 3 squares behind) – the cow walks straight ahead, every turn.
  • In front (the 3 squares ahead) – the cow turns round and walks the opposite way.
  • At its side – the cow stands still (💤) until Max bumps into it, or another cow walks into it. No cow stays in one place for more than 5 turns, though: after that she wakes and wanders off in an open direction.

Cows out of Max's reach wander at random. The alien Attractor Beam picks a random square and pulses it for three turns (two later in the game). On the next turn it fires, and any cow still standing there is abducted. Then there's a one-turn pause (none near the end) and it picks a new square. As time passes the aliens get better at choosing squares with cows on them, and the cows come through the gate faster. Only one square is ever targeted at a time, and Max is quite safe, so nudge any cow off a pulsing square in time. The game ends when the last cow has left the grid, and your score is the number of cows you walked out of the right-hand side into the pasture.
